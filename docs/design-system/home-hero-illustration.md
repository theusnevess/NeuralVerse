# Home Hero Editorial SVG Artwork

## NV-600.9-R5

The Home hero illustration is a decorative editorial SVG composed inline so it can inherit NeuralVerse design tokens, z-index layering, and reduced-motion behavior from the application stylesheet.

## Purpose

The artwork creates a right-side research observatory field that suggests semantic topology, scientific instrumentation, and knowledge navigation without competing with the Home content.

## Composition

- Left side remains negative space for logo, title, description, and CTAs.
- Right side carries the semantic network concentration.
- Micro fragments extend toward the center without covering content.
- The silhouette follows a diagonal flow from center-right toward upper/right regions.

## Layer System

1. Technical substrate: faint grid fragments and calibration marks.
2. Semantic microstructure: tiny latent particles and sparse semantic markers.
3. Primary knowledge topology: right-side node network with short and medium edges.
4. Observatory instrumentation: small alignment marks and a rare signal pulse.

## Motion

Motion is limited to a fade-in, subtle anchor-node breathing, and one rare signal pulse. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, all animation is disabled.

## Accessibility

The illustration is decorative only. It is rendered with `aria-hidden`, `focusable=false`, `pointer-events: none`, and `user-select: none`.

## Forbidden Regressions

Do not reintroduce radar discs, graph wallpaper, large blobs, random full-screen particles, or interactive behavior.
