# The Magnifying Card Analogy

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Convolution Intuition topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on sliding magnifier filters.

### explanation

Imagine holding a small magnifying card (kernel) over a large page of text. You slide the card from left to right, line by line (stride). The card highlights a small window of characters, blends them together according to a formula, and writes the summary on a new notepad. The notepad is the convolved output.
