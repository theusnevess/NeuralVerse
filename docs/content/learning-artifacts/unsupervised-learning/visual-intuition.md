# Sorting Blocks in the Dark

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Unsupervised Learning topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy for unsupervised sorting based on physical shapes.

### explanation

Imagine sorting a pile of colored blocks in a dark room. You don't have labels telling you what is what, but you can feel their shapes and group them into piles of spheres, cubes, and pyramids based on their features. You are discovering natural clusters without a supervisor.
