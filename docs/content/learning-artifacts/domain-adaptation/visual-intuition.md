# The Specialized Doctor's Desk

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Domain Adaptation and Specialization topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on studying a field vs consulting books during work.

### explanation

Imagine hiring a general doctor to work in a specialized cardiology clinic. Domain adaptation is like sending them to study cardiology medical journals and practice cardiac diagnoses (fine-tuning). However, even after studying, they still keep medical textbooks on their desk during patient visits to double-check drug dosages and rare cases (RAG).

## Optional Enrichment Fields

### motivation

Understanding Fine-Tuning and Adaptation is critical for specializing models for custom tasks, aligning generation safety, deploying LoRA adapters in production, and selecting RAG combinations.
