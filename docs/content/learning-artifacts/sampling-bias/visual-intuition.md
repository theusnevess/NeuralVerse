# The Basketball Team Height Skew

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Sampling and Sampling Bias topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Develop spatial intuition for skew in non-random convenience groups.

### explanation

Imagine trying to find the average height of people in a city, but you only collect height samples from the university basketball team. Your sample average will be skewed much higher than the city's true average. The team is a biased sample that does not represent the whole population.
