## Artifact Summary

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Compare random, stratified, and convenience sampling across bias risks and uses.

### explanation

| Sampling Method | How it Works | Bias Risk | Complexity | Best Use Case |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Simple Random | Every member has equal chance | Low (if large) | Low | Standard homogenous datasets |
| Stratified | Sample proportionally from subgroups | Very Low | Medium | Multi-demographic / imbalanced classes |
| Convenience | Sample easily accessible data | High | Very Low | Quick prototyping (not for production) |
