# Sand Mound Analogy for Normal Distributions

## Artifact Summary

Uses analogy and mental models to build intuition about Sand Mound Analogy for Normal Distributions — maps familiar concepts to the technical mechanics of Probability Distributions, making abstract ideas concrete.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide physical analogies for bell-shaped mounds vs. flat sheets.

### explanation

Imagine dropping sand through a funnel. If you drop it in the center, it forms a mound (Normal distribution: most points are near the average). If you spread the sand evenly across a flat tray, it forms a flat sheet (Uniform distribution: equal probability everywhere).
