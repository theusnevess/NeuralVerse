# Highlighted Articles vs. Causal Writers

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Encoder vs Decoder Architectures topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on highlights vs incremental generation.

### explanation

An encoder is like a researcher reading a complete article and highlighting key sections (unconstrained context). A decoder is like a writer typing a story letter-by-letter; they can look back at what they wrote, but they cannot see the future chapters that haven't been written yet (causal masking).

## Optional Enrichment Fields

### motivation

Understanding Transformers is critical for building modern LLMs (GPT-4, Llama), semantic search retrievers, RAG interfaces, and Vision Transformers.
