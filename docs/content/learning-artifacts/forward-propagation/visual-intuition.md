# Conveyor Belt Stations Analogy

## Artifact Summary

Uses analogy and mental models to build intuition about Conveyor Belt Stations Analogy — maps familiar concepts to the technical mechanics of Forward Propagation, making abstract ideas concrete.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on assembly line transformation stations.

### explanation

Imagine a sequence of manufacturing conveyor belts. The raw materials (inputs) enter the first station, where they are mixed and pressed (matrix product + bias). The result is sent to the next station for chemical treatment (activation function). This repeats until the finished product (prediction) exits the final station.
