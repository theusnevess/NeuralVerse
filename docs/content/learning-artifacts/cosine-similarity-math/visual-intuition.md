# Length Invariance of Cosine Similarity

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Cosine Similarity topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Develop spatial intuition for vectors mapping onto a unit circle.

### explanation

Imagine two arrows pointing from the origin. If you stretch the arrows to be 10 times longer (increasing magnitude), they still point in the exact same directions. Cosine similarity only cares about the opening angle between the two arrows, ignoring how far they stretch.
