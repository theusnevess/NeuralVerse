# Visual Tube Branches

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Receptive Fields topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on branched viewing scopes.

### explanation

Imagine looking at the world through a sequence of tubes. The first tube is thin, allowing you to see only one blade of grass (layer 1). If you join multiple tubes together in a branching tree, the viewer at the trunk can see the entire lawn (deep layer), even though each individual node only connects to its immediate branches.
