# Cookie Cutter Overlaps

## Artifact Summary

Uses analogy and mental models to build intuition about Cookie Cutter Overlaps — maps familiar concepts to the technical mechanics of Intersection over Union (IoU), making abstract ideas concrete.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on overlapping cut alignments.

### explanation

Imagine sliding a cookie cutter over a piece of dough that already has a cut mark. If your cutter overlaps exactly, you have perfect union and intersection (IoU = 1.0). If you miss the mark completely, you have no overlap (IoU = 0.0). The closer you align them, the more the overlap matches the total space combined.
