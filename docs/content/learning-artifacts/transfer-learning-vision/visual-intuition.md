# The Professional Illustrator

## Artifact Summary

Uses analogy and mental models to build intuition about The Professional Illustrator — maps familiar concepts to the technical mechanics of Transfer Learning in Vision, making abstract ideas concrete.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on adapting expert drawing skill sets.

### explanation

Imagine hiring a professional illustrator. They already spent years learning how to draw lines, shading, anatomy, and lighting (pretrained features). You don't need to teach them how to hold a pencil; you only need to show them a few sketches of your specific character for them to adapt their style (transfer learning).
