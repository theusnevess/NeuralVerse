# The Mosaic Tile Wall

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Digital Images and Pixel Representation topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on solid-color tile coordinates.

### explanation

Imagine a mosaic tile wall. From a distance, you see a smooth portrait. As you walk closer, you realize the image is made of thousands of tiny individual square tiles (pixels). Each tile has a single, solid shade of gray. The collection of these solid tiles creates the illusion of continuous gradients.
