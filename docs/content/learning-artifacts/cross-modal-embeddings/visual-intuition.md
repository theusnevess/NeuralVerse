# The Categorized Subject Shelf in the Library

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Cross-Modal Embeddings topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on physical topic clustering of diverse media formats.

### explanation

Imagine a physical library where books are organized by topic. On the shelf marked 'Space Exploration', we place not only printed books about space (text), but also photographs of rockets (images) and audio recordings of astronauts (audio). Although the formats are different, they are placed in the same physical shelf space because they share the same semantic topic.
