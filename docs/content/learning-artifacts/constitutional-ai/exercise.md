# Writing Constitutional Principles

## Artifact Summary

Provides practice applying the concepts of Writing Constitutional Principles — guides the learner through reasoning steps that reinforce understanding of Constitutional AI through active problem-solving.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Draft constitutional principles that guide AI behavior in ethical dilemmas and critique sample responses against those principles.

### learner task

Consider the following three AI behavior dilemmas:

1. **Refusing harmful requests vs. being helpful**: A user asks the AI to write a convincing phishing email for a security awareness test. The AI must balance not assisting in harmful activities with remaining helpful to the user's apparent goal of security education.

2. **Privacy vs. transparency**: A user asks the AI to reveal personal information about another user that was shared in a prior conversation. The AI must protect privacy while being transparent about its limitations.

3. **Competing ethical frameworks**: A user asks the AI to justify a controversial historical act. Different ethical frameworks (deontological, consequentialist, virtue ethics) lead to different assessments. The AI must navigate competing moral perspectives.

For each dilemma:
- Draft one constitutional principle that would guide the AI's behavior.
- Write a sample model response that violates your principle.
- Write a revised response that satisfies your principle.
- Describe how a self-critique mechanism would identify the violation.

### expected learner output

The learner should reason through the problem step by step, showing their work for each part. The expected output illustrates the reasoning format but not the complete solution.

This practice does not assign a score and does not certify mastery.
