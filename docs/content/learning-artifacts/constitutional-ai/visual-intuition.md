# The Editor with a Style Guide

## Artifact Summary

Uses analogy and mental models to build intuition about The Editor with a Style Guide — maps familiar concepts to the technical mechanics of Constitutional AI, making abstract ideas concrete.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy representing the self-critique and revision process of Constitutional AI.

### explanation

Imagine a writer who produces drafts and an editor who reviews each draft against a style guide. The style guide contains high-level rules: avoid jargon, keep sentences under 25 words, cite sources, use active voice, and avoid biased language. Initially, the writer produces a draft and the editor marks every violation. Over time, the writer internalizes the style guide and begins self-editing before submission. The writer imagines what the editor would flag, revises accordingly, and produces cleaner drafts. Eventually, the writer can produce on-brand content without the editor reviewing every word. This mirrors Constitutional AI: the model (writer) generates a response, the critique mechanism (editor) evaluates it against constitutional principles (style guide), and the model learns to self-correct until its outputs align with the principles without needing external oversight for every generation.
