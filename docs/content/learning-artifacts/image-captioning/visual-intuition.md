# The Commentary Eyes Tracking the Replay

## Artifact Summary

Uses analogy and mental models to build intuition about The Commentary Eyes Tracking the Replay — maps familiar concepts to the technical mechanics of Image Captioning, making abstract ideas concrete.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy representing visual focus shifting during continuous speech.

### explanation

Imagine a sports commentator watching a slow-motion replay of a goal. As they describe the play ('He kicks... the ball... into the net'), their eyes trace the player's foot, then follow the ball, and finally focus on the goal net. Their verbal description (text generation) is constantly guided by where they are looking on the screen (visual attention).
