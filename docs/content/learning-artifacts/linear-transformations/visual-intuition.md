# Visualizing Space Stretching and Rotations

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Linear Transformations topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide a spatial analogy of a grid sheet morphing under linear mappings.

### explanation

Imagine a rubber sheet with a grid drawn on it. A linear transformation is like stretching, rotating, or squeezing the sheet. The origin (0,0) stays fixed, and all grid lines remain straight and parallel. Every point on the sheet moves to a new coordinate, representing a change of basis.
