# Multimodal Transformers and Question-Guided Attention Grids

## Artifact Summary

Covers Multimodal Transformers and Question-Guided Attention Grids within the broader topic of Visual Question Answering — explains the core ideas, their role in AI systems, and what makes them important for practitioners to understand.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Explain joint text-image encoders, question embedding alignments, attention grids, and reasoning pipelines.

### explanation

Visual Question Answering (VQA) requires an AI system to provide a natural language answer to a query about a given image (e.g., 'How many cars are parked under the tree?'). This task requires complex multimodal reasoning, combining visual scene understanding (object detection, spatial relations, attributes) with textual semantic parsing. Modern models solve VQA by tokenizing both the question and the image, feeding them into a shared multimodal Transformer or cross-attention layer, and allowing the model to attend to both the text tokens of the question and the visual grids of the image to output the answer.
