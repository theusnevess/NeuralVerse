# The Detective Reading the Crime Scene Photo

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Visual Question Answering topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on targeted inspection of details to answer queries.

### explanation

Imagine a detective looking at a crime scene photo. Someone asks them, 'Is the window broken?'. The detective doesn't just scan the whole photo randomly; their brain uses the question to guide their eyes directly to the window in the photo, examine its details, and answer 'Yes, it is.'
