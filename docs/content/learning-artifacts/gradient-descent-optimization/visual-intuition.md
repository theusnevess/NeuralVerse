# Sledding Down a Snowy Bowl

## Artifact Summary

Uses analogy and mental models to build intuition about Sledding Down a Snowy Bowl — maps familiar concepts to the technical mechanics of Gradient Descent and Optimization, making abstract ideas concrete.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy for optimization steps and adaptive boosters.

### explanation

Imagine sliding down a snowy bowl on a sled. Batch gradient descent is checking the slope of the entire hill before taking a slow, cautious step. SGD is taking quick, jittery steps based on the slope immediately in front of you. Adam is like having rocket boosters that automatically adjust their thrust depending on how fast you are accelerating and how bumpy the snow is.

## Optional Enrichment Fields

### motivation

Deep learning builds on these core mechanisms — understanding them is essential for designing, debugging, and improving neural architectures.
