# The Investigative Detectives

## Artifact Summary

Uses analogy and mental models to build intuition about The Investigative Detectives — maps familiar concepts to the technical mechanics of Multi-Head Attention, making abstract ideas concrete.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on splitting observation jobs.

### explanation

Imagine reading a detective novel. If you try to track the suspects, the locations, and the timeline all at once, you might get confused. Instead, you divide the task among three detectives: Detective A tracks suspect names, Detective B tracks timestamps, and Detective C tracks map coordinates. At the end, you compile all their reports (concatenation) to solve the mystery.

## Optional Enrichment Fields

### motivation

Transformers form the backbone of modern LLMs — understanding their attention mechanisms, scaling properties, and architectural variants is essential for working with models like GPT-4, Llama, and their successors.
