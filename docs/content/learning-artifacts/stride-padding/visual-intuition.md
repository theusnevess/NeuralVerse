# Tiling Floor Spaces

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Stride and Padding topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on custom-cut spacer blocks.

### explanation

Imagine tiling a bathroom floor. Stride is the width of your tiles. If your stride matches the tile width, they fit neatly. If your stride is larger, you leave gaps. Padding is like adding spacer blocks at the edges of the room to ensure you can fit complete tiles up to the walls without cutting them.
