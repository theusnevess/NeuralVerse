# Footnotes in Translations

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Encoder–Decoder Architectures topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on footnotes restoring details to summarized texts.

### explanation

Imagine translating a detailed book into a summary, then expanding that summary back into a full-sized textbook. Without notes, the expanded version would be vague and lack detail. Skip connections are like copying specific page footnotes from the original book directly into the final draft, ensuring local facts aren't lost.
