# Dense vs. Sparse Representations

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Embedding Models topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Develop intuition on dense semantic spaces vs. sparse keyword lists.

### explanation

Imagine looking at an apple. A sparse representation is like a checklist of exact words: 'red', 'round', 'sweet'. A dense representation is like a coordinates point in a color-space, shape-space, and taste-space. The dense point captures that a pear is similar to an apple even if they share no letters, while the checklist is highly specific to the words used.
