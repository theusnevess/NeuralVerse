# Dimensionality Reduction Concepts

## Artifact Summary

Covers Dimensionality Reduction Concepts within the broader topic of Dimensionality Reduction — explains the core ideas, their role in AI systems, and what makes them important for practitioners to understand.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Explain the curse of dimensionality and compare PCA, t-SNE, and UMAP.

### explanation

High-dimensional spaces suffer from the 'curse of dimensionality'—points become sparse and distances between them tend to converge. Dimensionality reduction maps high-dimensional vectors to lower-dimensional spaces (e.g., 2D or 3D for visualization) while preserving as much structure as possible. Strategies include: 1. Principal Component Analysis (PCA): A linear method that projects data onto directions of maximum variance. 2. t-SNE and UMAP: Non-linear neighborhood-preserving methods that keep close points together, making them ideal for visualizing word embedding clusters.
