# Comparing Projection Methods

## Artifact Summary

Provides practice applying the concepts of Comparing Projection Methods — guides the learner through reasoning steps that reinforce understanding of Dimensionality Reduction through active problem-solving.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Compare linear and non-linear dimensionality reduction algorithms for embeddings.

### learner task

Explain the difference between PCA (linear) and t-SNE (non-linear) in terms of what they preserve, and explain why t-SNE is preferred for visualizing embedding clusters.

### expected learner output

The learner should reason through the problem step by step, showing their work for each part. The expected output illustrates the reasoning format but not the complete solution.
