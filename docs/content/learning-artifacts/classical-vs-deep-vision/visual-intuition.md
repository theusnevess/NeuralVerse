# Manual Rules vs. Mass Training

## Artifact Summary

Uses analogy and mental models to build intuition about Manual Rules vs. Mass Training — maps familiar concepts to the technical mechanics of Classical Computer Vision vs Deep Learning Vision, making abstract ideas concrete.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on custom carpenter criteria vs. automated pattern checks.

### explanation

Imagine building a automated wood sorter. Classical vision is hiring a carpenter to write down rules about wood grain patterns and knots (manual features). Deep learning is showing a machine 10,000 photos of good and bad wood, allowing it to figure out its own criteria for sorting.
