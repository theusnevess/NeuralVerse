# Post Office Cardboard Boxes

## Artifact Summary

Uses analogy and mental models to build intuition about Post Office Cardboard Boxes — maps familiar concepts to the technical mechanics of Anchor-Based vs Anchor-Free Detection, making abstract ideas concrete.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on template boxes vs custom wrap alignments.

### explanation

Imagine a post office sorting boxes. Anchor-based sorting is providing the staff with 9 pre-sized cardboard boxes and asking them to crop items to fit the closest box size (predicting offsets). Anchor-free sorting is letting the staff custom-wrap every item by wrapping paper directly around its dimensions from its center.
