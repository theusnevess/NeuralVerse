# Seat Assignment Tickets

## Artifact Summary

Uses analogy and mental models to build intuition about Seat Assignment Tickets — maps familiar concepts to the technical mechanics of Positional Encoding, making abstract ideas concrete.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on stamped seat cards.

### explanation

Imagine a group of students entering a classroom. If they sit anywhere without assignments, the teacher cannot track who is sitting where. Positional encoding is like handing each student a stamped ticket with their seat number as they enter. They add this ticket to their ID badge (vector addition), ensuring they can always identify who is sitting next to whom.
