# Storytelling Word-Association Game

## Artifact Summary

Uses analogy and mental models to build intuition about Storytelling Word-Association Game — maps familiar concepts to the technical mechanics of Autoregressive Generation, making abstract ideas concrete.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on incremental word chains.

### explanation

Imagine playing a game of word-association where you must continue a story one word at a time. Each time you add a word, you look at the entire story written so far to decide what makes the most sense next. If you want a predictable story, you choose the most obvious word. If you want a creative story, you roll a die to pick from a list of reasonable alternatives.
