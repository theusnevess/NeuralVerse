# Target Analogy for Bias vs. Variance

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Bias–Variance Tradeoff topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Develop spatial intuition for systematic deviations vs. scatter spreads.

### explanation

Imagine a target. Bias is how far the center of your shot cluster is from the bullseye (systematic deviation). Variance is the scatter radius of the shots (instability). An optimal model strikes a balance, hitting near the center with a tight cluster, rather than being centered but scattered (high variance) or grouped but far off (high bias).
