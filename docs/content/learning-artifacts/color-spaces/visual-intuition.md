# Stacked Colored Panes Analogy

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Image Channels and Color Spaces topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on superimposed glass overlays.

### explanation

Imagine three colored panes of glass—one red, one green, one blue. If you stack them together and shine a white light through them, they combine to project a full-color slide. Pulling the slide apart into three separate sheets shows the independent contributions of each primary color channel.
