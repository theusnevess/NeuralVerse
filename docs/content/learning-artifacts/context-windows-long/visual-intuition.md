# Study Desks and Page Scans

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Context Windows and Long Context topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on desk layouts and visual searches.

### explanation

Imagine study prep for an exam. You have a desk (context window) where you lay out reference books. A small desk only fits one book; a massive desk fits fifty. However, the larger the desk, the longer it takes your eyes to scan all the open pages (quadratic complexity), and you might miss a single footnote hidden in the middle of page 40 (needle in a haystack).
