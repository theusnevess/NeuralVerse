# Dictionary Reading Analogy

## Artifact Summary

Uses analogy and mental models to build intuition about Dictionary Reading Analogy — maps familiar concepts to the technical mechanics of Epochs, Batches, and Learning Rate, making abstract ideas concrete.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on reading massive dictionaries.

### explanation

Imagine reading a massive dictionary. An epoch is reading the entire book from A to Z. A batch is reading one page at a time. The learning rate is how much you change your vocabulary understanding after finishing each page. If you change too much, you confuse words. If you change too little, you learn nothing.
