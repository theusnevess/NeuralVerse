# Multimodal Fusion and Cross-Attention Projection

## Artifact Summary

Covers Multimodal Fusion and Cross-Attention Projection within the broader topic of Vision-Language Models — explains the core ideas, their role in AI systems, and what makes them important for practitioners to understand.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Explain vision encoders, language decoders, projection alignment layers, and visual token processing.

### explanation

Vision-Language Models (VLMs) bridge computer vision and natural language processing by enabling a single neural network architecture to process both visual inputs and textual descriptions. Modern VLMs typically consist of a vision encoder (like a ViT or CNN) and a language decoder (like a Transformer LLM). A projection layer (often a simple MLP or linear projection) maps the vision encoder's visual token embeddings into the same dimensional space as the language model's text token embeddings, allowing the language model to process visual tokens as if they were words in a prompt.
