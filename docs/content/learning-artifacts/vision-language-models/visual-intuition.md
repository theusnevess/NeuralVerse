# The Bilingual Translator at the Conference

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Vision-Language Models topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy representing modality translation to a shared decoder language.

### explanation

Imagine a bilingual meeting. One person only speaks English (the LLM) and the other only speaks visual sign language (the Vision Encoder). The projection layer acts as a real-time translator: it translates the visual hand signs into English words and inserts them directly into the English speaker's conversation transcript, enabling them to understand the signs and respond in English.
