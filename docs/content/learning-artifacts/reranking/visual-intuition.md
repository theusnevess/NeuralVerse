# Reranker Analogy

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Reranking topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide a filtering analogy for multi-stage search architectures.

### explanation

Imagine a company hiring. Stage 1 is the HR filter: they scan resumes for keywords and basic skills to quickly narrow down 10,000 applicants to 20 candidates. Stage 2 is the technical interview: the team spends hours with each of the 20 candidates to find the top 2. HR is the Bi-Encoder (fast, rough filter); the team is the Cross-Encoder (slow, deep filter).
