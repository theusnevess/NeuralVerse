# Gold Mining Scouts

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the One-Stage vs Two-Stage Detectors topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on scouting dig sites vs grid filtration.

### explanation

Imagine a gold mining team. A two-stage process is first having scouts survey the land and mark a few potential dig sites (region proposals), then sending heavy machinery to extract gold only from those marked areas. A one-stage process is setting up a massive grid of sieves across the entire riverbed and filtering all segments simultaneously in one single sweep.
