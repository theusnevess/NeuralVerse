# The Post Office Sorting Office

## Artifact Summary

Uses analogy and mental models to build intuition about The Post Office Sorting Office — maps familiar concepts to the technical mechanics of Query Routing and Intent Detection, making abstract ideas concrete.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy representing mail routing based on label classification categories.

### explanation

Imagine a sorting office at a postal company. A mail handler reads the address and category on each envelope: packages go to the shipping bay, local mail goes to the delivery trucks, and international mail goes to the airport. Without this central sorting, all packages would be sent on delivery trucks, leading to delays and inefficient handling.
