# The Recipe Reader vs. the Line Chef

## Artifact Summary

Uses analogy and mental models to build intuition about The Recipe Reader vs. the Line Chef — maps familiar concepts to the technical mechanics of Agentic AI Fundamentals, making abstract ideas concrete.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on static reading vs active tasting and adjusting.

### explanation

Imagine a recipe reader vs. a line chef. A static LLM is like the reader: they read the recipe out loud and stop. An agent is like the chef: they read the recipe, check if they have onions, cut them, put them in the pan, observe if the pan is too hot, adjust the heat, and taste the food to decide when it's done.
