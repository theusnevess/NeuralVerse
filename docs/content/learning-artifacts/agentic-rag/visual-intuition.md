# The Student Writing a Research Paper

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Agentic Retrieval Systems topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy representing dynamic search iterations during drafting.

### explanation

Imagine a student writing a research paper. Instead of printing five articles from the library, reading them, writing the paper, and turning it in immediately (static RAG), the student writes a section, realizes they need a specific statistic, goes back to the library to find a new article, rewrites their section based on the new data, and verifies their facts before submission (agentic RAG).
