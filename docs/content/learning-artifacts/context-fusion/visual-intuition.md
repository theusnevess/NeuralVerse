# The Unified Critic Scoreboard

## Artifact Summary

Uses analogy and mental models to build intuition about The Unified Critic Scoreboard — maps familiar concepts to the technical mechanics of Context Fusion and Aggregation, making abstract ideas concrete.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on rank-based point systems to merge distinct lists.

### explanation

Imagine a group of three movie critics, each providing a top-10 list of films. Instead of arguing about individual rating scores, you compile a master list by assigning points based on how high each film ranks on their lists (1st place gets 60 points, 2nd gets 30, etc.). Films that appear in the top spots of multiple lists naturally float to the top of your master recommendation list.
