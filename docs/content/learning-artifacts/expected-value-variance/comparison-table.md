### explanation

| Metric | Mathematical Meaning | Physical / Visual Analogy | AI Engineering Significance |
|---|---|---|---|

| Variance | Spread of data | Scatter range | Model stability, error dispersion |
| Std Deviation | Average distance from mean | Average error radius | Uncertainty intervals, scale scaling |
