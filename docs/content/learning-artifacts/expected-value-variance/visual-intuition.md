# Target Analogy for Bias and Variance

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Expected Value and Variance topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Develop spatial intuition for prediction hits on targets representing bias vs. variance.

### explanation

Imagine shooting arrows at a target. The expected value is the average center of your hits. Variance is how spread out the arrow holes are. If all arrows hit a tight cluster but far from the center, you have high bias and low variance. If they are centered but widely scattered, you have low bias and high variance.
