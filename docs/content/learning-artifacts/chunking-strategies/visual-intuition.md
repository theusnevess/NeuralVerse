# Visualizing Chunking Boundaries

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Chunking Strategies topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide a spatial analogy for fixed, recursive, and semantic chunking split points.

### explanation

Imagine a long scroll of paper containing a story. If you cut it with scissors every 10 inches (fixed size), you might slice in the middle of a word or sentence, losing context. If you cut only at paragraph breaks (recursive), each piece makes sense. If you read the scroll and only cut when the story changes from talking about 'dogs' to 'cooking recipes' (semantic), each chunk represents a single cohesive topic.
