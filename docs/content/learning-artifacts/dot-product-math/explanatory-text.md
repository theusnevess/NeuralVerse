# The Mathematical Dot Product

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Dot Product topic and serves as a Explanatory Text.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Explain the algebraic formula and geometric meaning of the dot product.

### explanation

The dot product (or inner product) multiplies matching elements of two vectors and sums the results: a . b = sum(a_i * b_i). Geometrically, the dot product combines vector magnitudes and the angle between them: a . b = ||a|| ||b|| cos(theta). It measures projection: how much of vector A points in the direction of vector B. If vectors are perpendicular, their dot product is 0. In neural networks, the dot product is the core operation of self-attention and dense layers, computing how strongly features align.
