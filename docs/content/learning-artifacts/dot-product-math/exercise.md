# Calculating Vector Dot Products

## Artifact Summary

Provides practice applying the concepts of Calculating Vector Dot Products — guides the learner through reasoning steps that reinforce understanding of Dot Product through active problem-solving.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Compute algebraic dot products and evaluate the angle relationship.

### learner task

You have two vectors: u = [2, 3] and v = [4, -1]. Calculate their dot product. Explain what the sign of the result indicates about the angle between the two vectors.

### expected learner output

The learner should reason through the problem step by step, showing their work for each part. The expected output illustrates the reasoning format but not the complete solution.
