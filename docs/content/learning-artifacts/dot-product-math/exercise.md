# Calculating Vector Dot Products

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Dot Product topic and serves as a Exercise.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Compute algebraic dot products and evaluate the angle relationship.

### learner task

You have two vectors: u = [2, 3] and v = [4, -1]. Calculate their dot product. Explain what the sign of the result indicates about the angle between the two vectors.

### expected learner output

u . v = (2 * 4) + (3 * -1) = 8 - 3 = 5. Since the dot product is positive, the angle between the two vectors is acute (between 0 and 90 degrees), indicating they point in generally similar directions.
