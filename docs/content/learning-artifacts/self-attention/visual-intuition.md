# Interest Cards and Badge Scans

## Artifact Summary

This artifact belongs to the Self-Attention Mechanism topic and serves as a Visual Intuition.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy based on classroom card matching.

### explanation

Imagine being in a crowded room where everyone is wearing a name tag with their interests (Keys) and holding a card listing what they want to talk about (Queries). To find the best conversation partners, you compare your card (Query) against everyone's name tags (Keys). Once you calculate the match scores (Attention scores), you listen to the actual ideas (Values) of the people with the highest matches, ignoring the rest.
