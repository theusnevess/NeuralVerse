# Stretching Checkerboards

## Artifact Summary

Uses analogy and mental models to build intuition about Stretching Checkerboards — maps familiar concepts to the technical mechanics of Image Resolution, Sampling, and Resizing, making abstract ideas concrete.

## Required Contract Fields

### objective

Provide an analogy for interpolation boundaries based on rubber grid stretches.

### explanation

Imagine stretching a small rubber checkerboard. To make it larger, you must insert new squares. If you just duplicate the nearest square (nearest neighbor), the board looks blocky. If you blend the colors of surrounding squares (bilinear), the transition is smooth but slightly blurry.
