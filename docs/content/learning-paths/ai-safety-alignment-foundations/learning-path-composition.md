# AI Safety and Alignment Foundations — Learning Path Composition

## 1. Purpose

This Learning Path organizes multiple Modules into a coherent, high-level AI Safety and Alignment curriculum progression.

It serves as an organizational and navigational guide for learners. The Learning Path does not duplicate, embed, or rewrite the content of the referenced modules, lessons, or learning artifacts.

## 2. Learning Path Aim

Introduce learners to comprehensive AI safety and alignment concepts, including prompt injection attacks and defenses, jailbreak techniques and refusal bypass, grounding and verification strategies for output reliability, Constitutional AI principles and mechanisms, multi-layer guardrail architectures for production LLM systems, and policy enforcement and output validation frameworks covering structural and semantic validation, approval chains, and safe degradation strategies.

This path focuses on equipping learners with the conceptual tools to design, evaluate, and operate safe LLM applications, without claiming or certifying competency mastery.

## 3. Included Modules

### 3.1 AI Safety Fundamentals

*   **Module ID:** `module-ai-safety-fundamentals`
*   **Location:** `docs/content/modules/ai-safety-fundamentals/module-composition.md`
*   **Pedagogical Role:** Establishes foundational safety concepts: injection attacks, jailbreak techniques, and grounding verification.
*   **Relationship to Learning Path Aim:** Satisfies the safety fundamentals step of the learning path's aim.

### 3.2 Alignment and Guardrails

*   **Module ID:** `module-alignment-guardrails`
*   **Location:** `docs/content/modules/alignment-guardrails/module-composition.md`
*   **Pedagogical Role:** Deepens safety concepts with constitutional alignment, guardrail architectures, and policy enforcement.
*   **Relationship to Learning Path Aim:** Covers the alignment and application-layer protection step of the learning path's aim.

This learning path does not duplicate any of the instructional content or text from the module compositions themselves.
