# Frontier AI Paradigms — Module Composition

## 1. Purpose

This module organizes lessons covering frontier AI research paradigms: world models and latent simulation, neurosymbolic AI, and AI for scientific discovery.

It provides an organizational boundary for these emerging paradigms without duplicating any instructional content.

## 2. Module Learning Aim

Introduce learners to emerging AI research paradigms, including world models and latent simulation for planning and reasoning, neurosymbolic AI bridging neural learning with symbolic reasoning, and AI-driven scientific discovery for hypothesis generation, surrogate simulation, and automated experimentation.

This module aims to connect paradigm-level concepts to practical understanding, without claiming or certifying competency mastery.

## 3. Included Lessons

### 3.1 World Models and Latent Simulation

*   **Lesson ID:** `lesson-world-models-and-latent-simulation`
*   **Location:** `docs/content/lessons/world-models-and-latent-simulation/lesson-composition.md`
*   **Pedagogical Role:** Teaches internal representations of environment dynamics, planning by simulation in latent spaces, and model-based approaches to reasoning and decision-making.
*   **Relationship to Module Aim:** Fulfills the world models and latent simulation requirement of the learning aim.

### 3.2 Neurosymbolic AI

*   **Lesson ID:** `lesson-neurosymbolic-ai`
*   **Location:** `docs/content/lessons/neurosymbolic-ai/lesson-composition.md`
*   **Pedagogical Role:** Details integration of neural learning with symbolic reasoning, differentiable programming, and hybrid architectures that combine connectionist and symbolic paradigms.
*   **Relationship to Module Aim:** Fulfills the neurosymbolic AI requirement of the learning aim.

### 3.3 AI for Scientific Discovery

*   **Lesson ID:** `lesson-ai-for-scientific-discovery`
*   **Location:** `docs/content/lessons/ai-for-scientific-discovery/lesson-composition.md`
*   **Pedagogical Role:** Focuses on hypothesis generation, surrogate simulation, automated experimentation, and AI-driven approaches to accelerating scientific research.
*   **Relationship to Module Aim:** Fulfills the AI for scientific discovery requirement of the learning aim.

This module composition does not duplicate any of the instructional content or text from the lesson compositions themselves.

The module structures lessons in the following sequence:
