# NeuralVerse — Glossary

## NeuralVerse

AI learning and research platform focused on structured study, retrieval, graphs, and AI engineering workflows.

## Retrieval Workspace

Area for search, memory, saved queries, pinned items, and knowledge trail.

## Knowledge Graph

Graph-based visualization of concepts, modules, relationships, and learning structure.

## Research Observatory

Core visual metaphor for the platform: scientific, precise, dark, minimal, and knowledge-oriented.

## React Island

A React/Vite-powered interactive section inside the otherwise static/frontend-first architecture.
