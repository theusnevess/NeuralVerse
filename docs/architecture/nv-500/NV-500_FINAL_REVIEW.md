## 3. Core Architectural Insights
*   **Insight 1:** `Retrieval complexity is primarily structural.`
    *   It is not classification complexity, metadata complexity, governance complexity, or lifecycle complexity. The challenge lies in coordinating fragmented lookup files under a unified authority.
*   **Insight 2 (Architectural Conservation Principle):**
    *   *A new concern does not justify a new foundation.*
    *   *A new foundation requires demonstrated insufficiency of existing foundations.*
