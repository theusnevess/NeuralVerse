Local Generative Assist

Optional, locally-powered generative assistance for explanations, summaries, and exploration.

About This Feature

Optional

The Local Generative Layer uses local LLMs (Ollama, llama.cpp, or OpenAI-compatible local servers) to provide optional explanations, summaries, and exploration suggestions.

Important: Generated outputs are non-canonical. Always verify with official NeuralVerse materials. No data leaves your local machine.

This feature is disabled by default. Enable it in Settings.
